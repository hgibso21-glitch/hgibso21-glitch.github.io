What: Marching Bass Drum

Marching bass drum combines rhythm, movement, listening, and teamwork with others. Usually having 5 people playing their individual parts in a coordinated manner to sound like a full musical phrase.

  • It provides a rhythmic foundation for an ensemble.
  • Players learn to listen and stay in time together.
  • It combines musical practice with marching and performance.

How: Getting Started

Everyones journery varies from person to person. I could name the things I would personally emphaises as a beginner.

  1. Learn the marching fundamentals and make sure you confortable enough with it to focus more of youre energy on the music and not the feet.
  2. Dont play something hard too fast. Also start slow and build up to make sure you dont develop bad habits.
  3. Spend a good chunck of your time practicing rhythyms as bass drums split them. Having a strong understanding of where each notes fits in the tempo helps all bass drummers agree on the split.
